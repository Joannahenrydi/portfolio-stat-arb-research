# Portfolio research protocol v1 — frozen 2026-09-20 before this run

Scope: daily US-listed ETF prototype; eventual 200–500-stock PIT universe is blocked on historical security master, sector history, delistings and data access. This run is exploratory, not evidence of deployable equity alpha. Existing repo already inspected 2025–2026; that interval is a temporal holdout, NOT a pristine blind test.

Data: Yahoo via yfinance for prototype; Alpaca raw/all bars when credentials become available. Fetch 2017-01-01 through 2026-09-18 inclusive. SPY supplies calendar and market return. Fixed research ETF list has selection/survivorship bias; dollar volume derived from Yahoo split-adjusted close/volume is only a proxy, not verified historical raw notional. No invented market cap or PIT sector labels. Static ETF sector groups are economic research labels. No missing-price forward fill. Missing held-asset returns invalidate the run. Current adjusted prices provide retrospective total-return proxies, not executable quotes or PIT corporate action records.

Universe: 22 ETFs in 11 economic groups; price proxy >=5, >=252 observations, >=95% coverage of trailing 60 SPY sessions, median 60-day dollar-volume proxy >=$1m; top 400 (prototype has only 22). All filters use data through decision close. A separate Alpaca collection accepts a user-supplied historical candidate list, never claims today's asset master is PIT.

Train: 2018–2022. Validation: 2023–2024. Temporal audit: 2025–2026-09-18. Warm-up: 2017. No random split. Parameters frozen below; rolling 126-session market and group regressions use only earlier returns. Kalman prior predicts current residual before update. No model selection on audit. All three families receive diagnostic reports; blend is sole predefined strategy candidate.

Alpha: 3-day residual reversal, Kalman market-beta/group-relative innovation reversal (q=1e-5, R=1e-4), and volume dislocation times residual reversal. Cross-sectional z scores clipped to +/-3; equal-weight blend. Monthly/annual performance constitutes rolling estimation audit, not newly independent trials. No claim of fitting expected returns: scores and inverse variance are heuristic ranking inputs.

Portfolio: inverse variance alpha ranking projected off ETF-group dummies and rolling market beta; net=0, each group=0 and beta exposure=0 numerically. Gross <=1, single name <=10%, one-way sum(abs(delta weights)) <=0.50 per rebalance, order notional <=0.1% trailing dollar ADV proxy on $100k NAV. If projection/constraint intersection cannot be reached by scaling from drifted holdings, liquidate with costs. No optimization of lambda after outcomes.

Timing: signal at t close, target at t+1 close, first earned close-to-close return t+1 to t+2. Drift holdings using asset returns before rebalancing; no same-close signal fills. This is conservative daily execution proxy, not an auction fill simulator. Charge initial entries and terminal liquidation. Borrow accrued calendar days. Forecast features and costs use prior-close observations at execution.

Costs per traded dollar: commission 0.5bp + assumed half-spread 2bp + slippage 1bp + 0.10*daily_vol*sqrt(order_notional/ADV). Borrow 3% annual on shorts; no hard-to-borrow availability assumed. Double all costs in stress. Base capital $100,000, no cash yield. Fees/spread/impact are assumptions pending quotes/fills, not observed estimates.

Acceptance fixed now: validation AND audit net Sharpe >=1, positive net return, max drawdown >=-10%, mean rank IC >0, >=252 observations, positive net in each full calendar year. Cost-double, extra one-session delay, 2/5-day residual horizons, every-other-day rebalance, remove best 5% validation contributors, and deterministic 20% universe removal must each preserve positive validation/audit return. Any data integrity failure or missing PIT/raw/action/borrow/execution validation blocks promotion regardless of statistical results. Reject => target cash, no orders. Do not retune after rejection.

Paper: only shadow target artifacts in v1; broker execution/reconciliation remains blocked pending data acceptance, credentials, borrow checks and paper validation. Log intended weights, reasons and zero approved targets. Future genuinely untouched evaluation begins after protocol freeze; 2–3 months of paper fills cannot be substituted with historical proxies.
